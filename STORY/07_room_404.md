# 07 — ROOM 404

**Genre:** Supernatural Horror
**Tone:** Quiet, sad, unsettling rather than shocking — grief wearing a haunted-house costume. Atmosphere over jump scares.
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Estimated playtime:** ~20 minutes
**One-line hook:** A hotel guest is handed a key to Room 404 — a room that isn't on any floor plan — and inside finds a perfect recreation of a childhood bedroom he doesn't remember having.

---

## PREMISE

Adrian Cole checks into a hotel the night before an important meeting, numb and distracted. The elevator behaves strangely. His key card is for Room 404 — but the front desk insists the hotel has no fourth floor of guest rooms, and no room 404 exists on any plan. Inside, the room is not a hotel room at all: old wallpaper, a single bed, a child's toy on the floor. Something — some *one* — is waiting for him to remember what happened the night his younger brother died.

## CAST

**Adrian Cole, 33 — Hotel Guest, Business Traveler**
Guarded, controlled, allergic to sentimentality. Successful on paper, hollow underneath.
*Motivation:* get through the night, close the deal tomorrow, keep moving.
*Fear:* remembering the night his younger brother Teddy died in a house fire when Adrian was 10.
*Secret:* he has always believed — silently, for 25 years — that the fire was his fault, because he was the one who left a door open that night.
*Relationships:* estranged-by-distance from his sister Sofia; has never told anyone, including partners, that he had a brother.

**Mrs. Leung, 68 — Night-Shift Concierge**
Warm, watchful, has worked this hotel for decades. Knows more than hotel policy would like.
*Motivation:* help Adrian face what the room is showing him, without scaring him into fleeing.
*Fear:* history repeating itself unresolved, the way it nearly did once before with another guest years ago.
*Secret:* she remembers this exact plot of land before the hotel was built — it's where the Cole family's house once stood, and Room 404 sits precisely where young Adrian's childhood bedroom was.

**Teddy (the Boy), apparition, age 7 (frozen)**
Not malicious. A memory-echo, not a ghost seeking revenge.
*Motivation (revealed late):* wants the true cause of the fire remembered correctly — not to torment Adrian, but to free him from 25 years of misplaced guilt.
*Relationships:* Adrian's little brother.

**Sofia Cole, 40 — Adrian's Older Sister (present only by phone)**
Practical, has processed the grief differently — by burying it in silence for Adrian's sake.
*Motivation:* protect Adrian, even now, even though the protection has cost them their closeness.
*Secret:* she has always known the fire was caused by faulty wiring in a space heater, not by any door Adrian left open — and never told him, believing a ten-year-old shouldn't carry either the guilt or the correction.

## CENTRAL CONFLICT
Why does Room 404 — a room that shouldn't exist — keep appearing to Adrian, and what does the recreated childhood bedroom want?

## HIDDEN TRUTH (GM ONLY)
Room 404 is a psychic echo tied to the physical location, surfacing now because Adrian is finally alone, still, and unguarded enough for suppressed grief to break through. Teddy's apparition isn't haunting Adrian out of anger — he's a fragment of memory trying to correct a story Adrian has told himself wrong for 25 years. The fire wasn't caused by a door Adrian left open; it was faulty wiring in an old space heater. Sofia has known this the whole time and hid it, thinking silence was mercy. Once Adrian faces the boy and hears the truth, the room lets him go.

## CHRONOLOGICAL TIMELINE
1. 25 years ago: the Cole family home stood where the hotel now stands.
2. 25 years ago: a faulty space heater sparks a fire at night; Teddy, age 7, dies.
3. 25 years ago: young Adrian, age 10, wrongly believes a door he left open let the fire spread.
4. Over the following decades: Sofia learns the true cause from the fire report but never corrects Adrian's belief.
5. Present, tonight: Adrian checks into a hotel built on that same ground, alone and distracted enough for the echo to surface.

## FORESHADOWING → PAYOFF MAP
- E4 (crayon drawing of fire) → E16 (room becomes the fire) — the child's drawing was always the true story.
- E6 (forgotten brother in photo) → E12 (the boy appears) — the photo names what's about to appear.
- E10 (burnt bear) / E11 (Mrs. Leung's recognition) → E14 (fire report) — objects carry real history.
- E13 (socket gesture) → E14 (space heater cause) — the boy was pointing at the truth the whole time.

## TWISTS
- **Minor reveal (E6):** Adrian had a brother he never mentions.
- **Minor reveal (E11):** Mrs. Leung has a personal history with this exact haunting.
- **Major twist (E14–15):** the fire wasn't Adrian's fault — it was faulty wiring, and his sister hid the truth to protect him.

## RECURRING OBJECTS / MOTIFS
- **The burnt teddy bear**: the emotional core object, tying toy → child → truth.
- **The crayon drawing**: foreshadows the climax visually before anyone understands it.
- **Wall socket / space heater**: a mundane object made quietly devastating.

## WHY IT'S FUN TO PLAY
The horror here isn't a monster to defeat — it's a truth to receive. Players spend the game assuming Adrian did something wrong, drawing increasingly ominous imagery, only to realize the "haunting" has been trying to forgive him the entire time. The final twist re-contextualizes every spooky drawing (the humming, the rearranging room, the boy in the doorway) as an act of care rather than menace — a rarer, more memorable horror beat than a jump scare.
