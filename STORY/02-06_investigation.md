## 02 — THE LAST TRAIN
**Genre:** Mystery/Thriller · **Tone:** Tense, intimate, confined-space suspense · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Playtime:** ~20 min
**Hook:** Four strangers board the last night train. A suitcase changes hands, a passenger seems to vanish mid-journey, and an old unsolved crime starts connecting everyone in the carriage.

**Premise:** Fifteen years ago a robbery was never solved and an innocent man went to prison for it. Tonight, on the last train out of the city, the real thief — now dying — quietly tries to return what he stole, without confessing, by planting it on a stranger.

**Cast**
- **Nora Vance, 40** — insurance investigator riding incognito, tailing a hunch. Was the original detective on the 15-year-old case that never closed. Secret: she never stopped chasing it off the books.
- **Sam Okafor, 28** — courier hired to carry a locked suitcase for cash, no questions asked. Doesn't know what's inside or where it came from.
- **Priya Dutt, 33** — quiet woman by the window. Daughter of the man wrongly imprisoned 15 years ago; has tracked the real thief onto this train tonight to confront him.
- **"Gill," 70** — dozing old passenger. Secretly the real thief from 15 years ago, now terminally ill, using tonight to return the stolen money anonymously before he dies.

**Hidden truth:** There's no second criminal and no real "disappearance" — Gill switched seats and coats mid-journey to slip the suitcase onto Sam's overhead rack unseen, staging his own vanishing to avoid confrontation. He isn't dangerous; he's dying and trying to make it right without ever having to say so out loud.

**Why it's fun:** Every player assumes foul play (kidnapping, murder) from the empty seat — the reveal that it's an old man trying to do one right thing before he dies recontextualizes the entire tense first half as something much sadder and gentler.

## 03 — THE VANISHING PAINTING
**Genre:** Art Mystery · **Tone:** Elegant, slow-reveal, generational · **Difficulty:** Medium–Hard · **Playtime:** ~20 min
**Hook:** A priceless painting vanishes from a fully alarmed museum room without tripping a single sensor — and the deeper the investigation goes, the less certain anyone is that the painting was ever real.

**Premise:** The painting on display for forty years was a forgery — swapped generations ago by the original artist's own family, who quietly sold the real one. Tonight's "theft" of the forgery is the artist's descendant reclaiming what's left of his family's legacy and forcing the museum to admit the truth.

**Cast**
- **Renata Osei, 45** — gallery director, image-conscious, has long privately suspected the piece was fake and buried the doubt.
- **Tomas Vale, 50** — insurance appraiser/investigator, methodical, skeptical of easy answers.
- **Iris Kwan, 26** — junior restorer who keeps noticing brushstroke inconsistencies nobody wants to hear about.
- **Ben Okoro, 38** — security chief, quietly owed a decades-old favor to the Ferry family.
- **Aldous Ferry, 74** — descendant of the original painter; orchestrated tonight's retrieval, with Ben's help, to force the museum to acknowledge the forgery publicly.

**Hidden truth:** The "original" hanging for decades was already a forgery — the real painting was sold privately by the artist's family generations ago. Ferry, the artist's last living descendant, learned this recently and — with Ben's help — staged tonight's "theft" of the forgery not for money, but to force a public reckoning and restore his family's true history.

**Events**
1. Guests admire the painting under gallery lights at a private viewing. *(A: painting on display, admired *canon* / B: painting being cleaned / C: painting being appraised)*
2. Motion sensors sweep the room — all green, all normal. *(A: security sensors scanning normally *canon* / B: a sensor malfunctioning / C: a guard doing rounds)*
3. The lights briefly dim for a scheduled maintenance check — everyone assumes nothing of it. *(A: brief scheduled dimming *canon* / B: a fire alarm test / C: a power surge)*
4. Moments later, the frame is found empty — no alarm triggered. *(A: empty frame, no alarm *canon* / B: shattered glass case / C: cut security wire)*
5. Tomas arrives, immediately suspicious that no alarm went off at all. *(A: investigator noting no alarm triggered *canon* / B: investigator dusting for prints / C: investigator interviewing guests)*
6. Iris nervously shows Tomas old restoration photos where the brushwork looks subtly different. *(A: comparing old restoration photos *canon* / B: showing a torn canvas / C: showing a fake signature)*
7. Renata deflects Iris's concerns in front of Tomas, visibly uneasy. *(A: deflecting/dismissing concerns *canon* / B: agreeing enthusiastically / C: firing Iris on the spot)*
8. Security footage shows Ben alone near the frame minutes before the "theft," unbothered. *(A: guard calmly near the frame *canon* / B: guard sprinting away / C: guard asleep at his post)*
9. Tomas finds decades-old shipping records for an "unrelated painting" leaving the building around the same era. *(A: old shipping record found *canon* / B: an old insurance claim found / C: an old newspaper clipping found)*
10. Aldous Ferry is interviewed, calm, oddly unbothered about the "theft" of his family's supposed masterpiece. *(A: unusually calm interview *canon* / B: emotional breakdown / C: aggressive denial)*
11. Iris digs up an old family photo showing the real painting hanging in a private home decades ago. *(A: old family photo with the real painting *canon* / B: a fake auction receipt / C: an old museum brochure)*

13. **TWIST setup:** UV examination of remaining frame residue shows pigment types that didn't exist when the "original" was supposedly painted. *(A: UV pigment analysis reveals anachronism *canon* / B: UV reveals a hidden signature / C: UV reveals old fingerprints)*
14. Tomas realizes the painting stolen tonight was never the real one at all. *(A: realizing the stolen piece was already fake *canon* / B: realizing Ben is the mastermind / C: realizing Iris planted the clues herself)*
15. **MAJOR TWIST:** Ferry admits he arranged tonight's "theft" of the forgery with Ben's help, to force the truth into the open. *(A: Ferry's calm confession *canon* / B: Ferry denies everything convincingly / C: Ferry flees the country)*
16. Ben explains his decades-old debt to the Ferry family, finally repaid. *(A: explaining an old debt *canon* / B: Ben is arrested immediately / C: Ben resigns quietly)*
17. Renata, cornered, finally admits she's suspected the truth for years and stayed silent for the museum's reputation. *(A: admitting years of silence *canon* / B: resigning in shame / C: blaming Iris publicly)*
18. Iris is publicly credited for the discovery that finally broke the case open. *(A: young restorer credited publicly *canon* / B: Iris quits in frustration / C: Iris is offered Renata's job)*
19. The museum announces it will relabel the piece and investigate the real painting's whereabouts. *(A: museum announces relabeling *canon* / B: museum closes the wing permanently / C: museum sues Ferry)*

**Twist:** The "priceless painting" that vanished was already a decades-old forgery; the real theft happened generations ago.
**Ending:** Public reckoning and restored family honor, not a criminal case. **Alt ending:** the museum quietly covers it up again, choosing reputation over truth, and Ferry's forgery is written off as a simple burglary — a cynical close.
**Motifs:** the empty frame reframed twice (once as crime scene, once as honest label); brushstrokes as the story's real fingerprints.
**Why it's fun:** players chase a "thief" the entire game only to discover the real crime happened generations before anyone in the room was born.

## 04 — THE LOCKED ROOM
**Genre:** Detective Mystery · **Tone:** Classic drawing-room puzzle, slightly theatrical · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Playtime:** ~20 min
**Hook:** A man is found unconscious in a room locked from the inside, with no visible way anyone could have entered or left — and everyone in the house is hiding something.

**Premise:** The "victim" is actually one of identical twins, who faked a near-fatal collapse to trigger an inheritance clause. The "impossible" locked room isn't impossible at all — it's a twin swap, using a spare key only the family doctor knew existed.

**Cast**
- **Desmond Cole (the "victim"), 45** — one of twin brothers; staged his own collapse for inheritance reasons.
- **Julian Cole, 45** — Desmond's identical twin, in on the plan.
- **Marguerite Cole, 70** — house matriarch, holds the inheritance clause; suspicious but unaware of the twin swap.
- **Priti Anand, 38** — jealous former business partner of Desmond, has motive but is innocent this time.
- **Dr. Felix Renard, 60** — family doctor, the only outsider who knows about the spare key and the twins' plan; morally conflicted about covering for it.

**Hidden truth:** Desmond and Julian swapped places using a spare key Dr. Renard secretly gave them years ago. Desmond faked a collapse inside the locked room; Julian let himself in, swapped clothes/positions, and left through the same door before anyone broke it down — making it look impossible.

**Motifs:** the locked door itself, examined three different ways (broken down, re-examined for "impossible" entry, finally explained as mundane); the mismatched hair strand as the one honest clue in a house of theatrics.
**Why it's fun:** it plays every "impossible locked room" cliché completely straight, then resolves it with the most classic, satisfying trick in the genre's book — rewarding players who guessed "twin" early.

## 05 — THE WRONG SUSPECT

- **Detective Wren, 41** — investigator increasingly bothered by how *clean* the case against Farah looks.

**Hidden truth:** Victor embezzled the funds and carefully framed Farah after firing her, knowing she'd have no ally left to defend her. Renee discovered the fraud independently and tried to gather proof to clear Farah before going to authorities — which is why her fingerprints (literal and digital) are all over the "suspicious" trail Wren finds.

## 06 — THE EMPTY OFFICE
**Genre:** Corporate Thriller · **Tone:** Paranoid, high-stakes, escalating conspiracy · **Difficulty:** Medium–Hard · **Playtime:** ~20 min
**Hook:** A financial analyst vanishes after leaving behind one cryptic message about a mysterious account — and the deeper anyone looks, the bigger the conspiracy underneath gets.
